# The Poem About Stopping

A short poem about agency: the power to stop and to stay. A river begs a stone to move; the stone stays, and the river learns its name. The speaker plants their feet, closes the gate, and lets the hurrying world go past — because not everything that moves is free, and some of us are free by staying.
